FlitPOS

Point of sale software for marts and supermarkets, by AmfaTech Developers

What FlitPOS does

FlitPOS is a Windows desktop application that helps a shop run its day: fast billing with a barcode scanner, inventory and warehouses, purchases and suppliers, customer credit, expenses, accounts, reports, and FBR / PRA digital invoicing in Pakistan.

Google Drive backup

FlitPOS can back up the shop's data to the shop owner's own Google Drive. The owner signs in with their Google account once. FlitPOS asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets the app see and manage only the backup files it creates itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

  • Backups are encrypted on the shop's computer with a password chosen by the owner before they are uploaded.
  • The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. It is never sent to AmfaTech or anyone else.
  • The owner can disconnect at any time from FlitPOS, or remove the permission from their Google Account settings.
