FlitPOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

FlitPOS ("the app") is point of sale software published by AmfaTech Developers ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with data, including data from a Google Account when the shop owner chooses to connect Google Drive.

1. Data the app handles

The app stores the shop's business data (items, bills, customers, suppliers, expenses, users) on the shop's own computer. We do not receive this data and do not operate a server that stores it.

2. Google user data

If the shop owner connects Google Drive, the app requests the following permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: to create backup files in the owner's Google Drive and to list and delete the backup files that the app itself created. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.
  • openid and email: only to show the owner which Google account is connected.

The app uses this access for one purpose: uploading, listing and removing the shop's own encrypted backup files. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not share it with anyone, and no person at AmfaTech reads it.

3. How Google data is stored and protected

  • Backup files are encrypted on the shop's computer, with a password the owner chooses, before they are uploaded. We cannot open them.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with the Windows credential store. It is never sent to us.
  • The app talks directly to Google's servers. No data passes through any server of ours.

4. Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Backups stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner or the app's retention setting deletes them. The owner can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time, which deletes the saved sign-in from the computer and revokes access. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the shop's data, the owner uses "Reset all data" in the app or removes the backup files from their Drive.

6. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
